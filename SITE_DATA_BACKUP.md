## ORGANIZATION OVERVIEW

**Organization Name**: Jeevika Foundation

**Tagline**: Building dignity, opportunity, and resilience alongside communities.

**Mission**:
Jeevika Foundation works alongside underserved communities to strengthen access to education, health, livelihoods, and grassroots leadership through patient, community-led programs.

**Story**:
1. Jeevika Foundation began with a simple belief: meaningful change lasts when communities are trusted, listened to, and supported over time.
2. What started as a grassroots effort has grown into a network of field teams, volunteers, educators, health workers, and local partners working across multiple districts.
3. Our work is designed for long-term capability, not short-term visibility. We respond to local priorities, strengthen local ownership, and stay committed beyond the first intervention.

## PROGRAMS

### 1. Digital Learning Centers
- **Summary**: Technology-enabled learning spaces that offer academic support, digital literacy, and mentorship for children and youth.
- **Outcomes**: 50+ centers supported, 200+ learners reached, Scholarship and mentoring pathways
- **Location**: Across 5 districts

### 2. Skills for Success
- **Summary**: Vocational and entrepreneurship training that improves employability and strengthens local income generation over time.
- **Outcomes**: 500+ participants trained, 60% linked to livelihoods, Enterprise guidance for women and youth
- **Location**: 10+ training hubs

### 3. Women's Empowerment Initiative
- **Summary**: Programs focused on financial literacy, self-help groups, confidence building, and leadership opportunities for women.
- **Outcomes**: 300+ women engaged, 50+ support groups formed, Leadership and safety workshops
- **Location**: Rural communities

### 4. Community Health Outreach
- **Summary**: Regular camps and awareness sessions that bring preventive care, basic health guidance, and referrals closer to underserved families.
- **Outcomes**: 1000+ monthly beneficiaries, Health camps and awareness drives, Local referral coordination

### 5. Rural Infrastructure Development
- **Summary**: Improving essential infrastructure such as learning spaces, toilets, water systems, and shared community facilities.

## FAQs

### 1. How can I volunteer with Jeevika Foundation?
You can contact us with your interests, skills, and availability. We align volunteers with suitable on-ground or remote opportunities.

### 2. Do you work only in one district?
No. Our programs span multiple districts, and we expand carefully where local partnerships and implementation capacity exist.

### 3. Can organizations collaborate with you?
Yes. We welcome collaboration with companies, educational institutions, healthcare providers, and grassroots groups.

### 4. How do you measure impact?
We track participation, outcomes, stories from the field, and program-level indicators to strengthen delivery and transparency.
